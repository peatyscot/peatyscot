Iodine is the medicinal register of [peat smoke]({{< relref "/flavours/peat-smoke" >}}):
antiseptic, TCP, sticking plaster, hospital corridor. It is the note that divides opinion on
[Islay]({{< relref "/regions/islay" >}}) whisky more than any other.

The cause is the peat itself. Coastal bogs form from seaweed and salt-laden vegetation, and
the phenolic compounds this produces skew towards the medicinal rather than the woody.

There is a better story than that, and it is told often: that the maturing casks breathe sea
air through the warehouse walls, year after year, until the whisky itself turns maritime. It
is a lovely idea with very little evidence behind it. The flavour is already there in the
new-make spirit, long before any warehouse has had the chance.

Found in [Lagavulin]({{< relref "/distilleries/lagavulin" >}}) and
[Ardbeg]({{< relref "/distilleries/ardbeg" >}}).
