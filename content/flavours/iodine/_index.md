Iodine is the medicinal register of [peat smoke]({{< relref "/flavours/peat-smoke" >}}):
antiseptic, TCP, sticking plaster, hospital corridor. It is the note that most divides
opinion on [Islay]({{< relref "/regions/islay" >}}) whisky.

The cause is the peat itself. Coastal bogs form from seaweed and salt-laden vegetation, and
the phenolic compounds this produces skew toward the medicinal rather than the woody. The
frequently repeated claim that maturing casks absorb sea air through the warehouse walls is
a good story with little evidence behind it — the flavour is present in the new-make spirit,
long before any warehouse.

Found in [Lagavulin]({{< relref "/distilleries/lagavulin" >}}) and
[Ardbeg]({{< relref "/distilleries/ardbeg" >}}).
