This site is intended for readers who are of legal drinking age in their country of
residence. That is 18 in the United Kingdom and much of Europe, 20 in Japan, and 21 in the
United States.

Whisky is typically 40–60% alcohol by volume. A 25 ml measure of a 46% whisky contains
roughly 1.15 UK units.

UK Chief Medical Officers' guidance is to drink no more than **14 units per week**, spread
over three or more days, with several drink-free days. There is no level of drinking that
can be described as completely safe.

Writing about whisky as a craft is not an argument for drinking more of it. If you are
concerned about your own drinking or someone else's, in the UK
[Drinkaware](https://www.drinkaware.co.uk/) offers free and confidential advice.

Never drink and drive.
