## Current status

**peaty.scot currently carries no advertising and no affiliate links.** This page describes
what will apply when it does, so that the policy is on record before there is any money
involved.

## Advertising

The site is intended to be funded by display advertising. Where ads appear they will be
clearly distinguishable from editorial content, limited in number, never placed above the
main content of a page, and never in interstitial or full-screen formats.

Advertisers have no input into what is written here. No page is commissioned, reviewed or
approved by a producer or retailer.

## Affiliate links

Where a link to a retailer earns a commission, it will be marked as such at the point of
the link, and the page will carry a disclosure. A commission does not change the price you
pay.

Commission arrangements do not influence which whiskies are covered, how they are
described, or what the flavour profiles say. Where a bottling is poor value or a producer
declines to disclose something material, this site says so regardless of whether a link on
that page earns anything.
