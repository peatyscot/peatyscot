advice.*

## What this site collects

peaty.scot is a static site. It has no accounts, no logins, no comment system, no
newsletter and no contact forms. **It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics.**

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard server request data — IP
address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — for delivery and security. This is handled
under Cloudflare's own privacy terms.

Your choice of whether to dismiss the age-affirmation notice is stored in your browser's
local storage. It never leaves your device and is not readable by this site's operator.

## Advertising

**No advertising is currently served.** When display advertising is enabled, this section
will apply and this page will be updated with the specifics before any ads run:

- Third-party ad providers may set cookies or use similar identifiers to serve and measure
  advertising.
- Readers in the UK and EEA will be asked for consent through a certified consent
  management platform before any non-essential identifiers are set, and may refuse or
  withdraw consent at any time.

## Your rights

Because the site holds no personal data of its own, there is no account to access, export
or delete. Requests concerning data processed by Cloudflare should be directed to
Cloudflare.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date. If advertising or analytics
are introduced, this page will say so before they go live rather than afterwards.
